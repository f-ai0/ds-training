"""
PROJECT PROBLEM STATEMENT
===========================

PROBLEM:
  Supervisors cannot easily tell which employees are at risk of absence,
  and attendance data sits in a raw punch-record spreadsheet nobody analyzes.

GOAL:
  Predict whether an employee will be absent (is_absent) on a given work day
  from their attendance pattern (punch behavior, position, historical timing),
  and give supervisors a dashboard to explore attendance trends and run
  predictions on demand.

WHO USES IT:
  Site supervisors and HR staff - non-technical users.

DATA:
  Practice_Dataset.xlsx - 360 daily attendance records for 30 employees
  (badge_number, name, position, work_date, in_time, out_time, punch_count,
  status). status = PRESENT/ABSENT is the raw label; is_absent is derived
  from it (1 = ABSENT, 0 = PRESENT). About 9.7% of records are ABSENT, so
  the target is imbalanced - this must be considered when choosing metrics
  and the baseline.

SUCCESS CRITERIA:
  - Model beats the majority-class baseline by a clear margin on the
    metrics that matter for imbalance (recall/F1/ROC-AUC on the ABSENT
    class - accuracy alone is misleading since "always predict PRESENT"
    already scores ~90%).
  - App runs from one command and returns a prediction in under 2 seconds.
  - A supervisor can use it without reading any code.

HONEST NOTE (Day 2 result):
  Using only historical/lag features (no same-day leakage), the tuned
  Logistic Regression reaches Test AUC ~0.55 and absent-class F1 ~0.21 -
  a small, real improvement over guessing, not a strong model. With 360
  rows / 30 employees / 16 days there simply isn't much history per
  employee to learn from yet. This is reported honestly rather than
  hidden - flag it to your supervisor and in the README's Limitations
  section (Task 5.1) as a data-volume limitation, not a bug.

OUT OF SCOPE:
  - Real-time integration with the badge system.
  - Predicting exact absence dates in advance (this predicts risk given a
    day's attendance pattern, not future dates).

NOTE ON CONFIRMATION:
  Per Task 1.1, this problem choice still needs to be confirmed with your
  supervisor before you continue past Day 1 - the dataset shape (attendance
  punches, no separate department/salary columns) differs a bit from the
  generic example in the task sheet, so flag that when you confirm.
"""
